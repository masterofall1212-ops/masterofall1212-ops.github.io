⚽ Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi is one of the most celebrated footballers in history. He is famous for his incredible dribbling, passing, goals and creativity on the football pitch.

Messi has represented Argentina throughout his international career and became a World Cup champion in 2022.

🏟️ Messi's Clubs

🔵🔴 FC Barcelona

Messi spent most of his professional career at Barcelona. He became one of the greatest players in the club's history and won numerous trophies with the team.

🔵🔴 Paris Saint-Germain

Messi joined Paris Saint-Germain in 2021 and played in Ligue 1 and the UEFA Champions League.

🩷🖤 Inter Miami CF

Messi joined Inter Miami in 2023 and continued his career in Major League Soccer.

🇦🇷 Argentina

Argentina has a rich football history and is one of the world's most famous football nations.

Messi has represented Argentina for many years and became a World Cup champion in 2022.

🏆 Argentina Trophies with Messi

🏆 Copa América 2021

Argentina defeated Brazil in the final at the Maracanã and Messi won his first major senior international trophy.

🏆 Finalissima 2022

Argentina defeated Italy at Wembley Stadium to win the Finalissima.

🏆 FIFA World Cup 2022

Argentina defeated France in the final in Qatar and Messi lifted the World Cup trophy.

🏆 Copa América 2024

Argentina won another Copa América title in 2024.

🏆 Major Achievements

🏆 8 Ballon d'Or Awards

Lionel Messi has won the Ballon d'Or 8 times, the most in the history of the award.

  • 🏆 2010 — FC Barcelona
  • 🏆 2011 — FC Barcelona

📖 Messi's Career Timeline

2004 — Barcelona Debut

Messi made his official first-team debut for FC Barcelona and began his incredible professional career.

2009 — First Ballon d'Or

Messi won his first Ballon d'Or and established himself among the world's leading footballers.

2012 — Record-Breaking Year

Messi produced one of the most remarkable goal-scoring years in football history.

2021 — Copa América Champion

Messi won his first major senior international trophy with Argentina at the Copa América.

2022 — World Cup Champion

Messi captained Argentina to the FIFA World Cup title in Qatar.

2023 — Inter Miami

Messi joined Inter Miami and began a new chapter of his career in Major League Soccer.

2024 — Copa América Champion Again

Argentina won another Copa América title, with Messi part of the championship-winning team.

🏆 FIFA World Cup 2022

Argentina's journey in Qatar became one of the most memorable chapters of Lionel Messi's international career.

🇦🇷 Group Stage
Argentina played Saudi Arabia, Mexico and Poland.
🇦🇺 Round of 16
Argentina defeated Australia and advanced to the quarter-finals.
🇳🇱 Quarter-final
Argentina faced the Netherlands and advanced after a penalty shootout.
🇭🇷 Semi-final
Argentina defeated Croatia 3–0 and reached the World Cup final.
🇫🇷 Final
Argentina faced France in a dramatic final and won the World Cup after a penalty shootout.

🏆 WORLD CHAMPIONS 🏆

ARGENTINA — QATAR 2022

Lionel Messi lifted the FIFA World Cup trophy and won the tournament's Golden Ball.
