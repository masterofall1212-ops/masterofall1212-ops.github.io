⚽ Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi is one of the most celebrated footballers in history. He is famous for his incredible dribbling, passing, goals and creativity on the football pitch.

Messi has represented Argentina throughout his international career and became a World Cup champion in 2022.

🇦🇷 Argentina

Argentina has a rich football history and is one of the world's most famous football nations.

In 2022, Argentina won the FIFA World Cup in Qatar, with Messi playing a central role in the team's campaign.

🏆 Major Achievements

🏆 FIFA World Cup — 2022
🏆 Copa América — 2021
🏆 Finalissima — 2022
🏆 Multiple Ballon d'Or awards
